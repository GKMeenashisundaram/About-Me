# About Me

Research portfolio, open-source projects, and engineering innovations in advanced manufacturing, materials, and AI-driven technologies.

## Profile

I am a **Senior Research Fellow and Project Lead** with 10+ years of international research experience in **additive manufacturing, AI-driven smart manufacturing, and advanced materials engineering** for aerospace, semiconductor, and biomedical applications.

I have independently conceived, secured, and led **S$4.8M+ in competitive research funding** as PI/Co-PI across six national and industry-funded projects in Singapore, advancing technologies from **TRL 2 to TRL 6/7** and translating research outcomes into industrial solutions.

My research contributions include **34 peer-reviewed publications (h-index: 26, i10-index: 30)** and **5 technology disclosures**. I was recognised for outstanding research achievement through Australia's **Global Talent Independent Visa (Subclass 858)**.

I am passionate about developing impactful technologies, mentoring researchers, and building strong academia–industry partnerships.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **AI-enabled advanced manufacturing**, integrating:

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning for intelligent process optimisation
- Additive manufacturing of metallic and ceramic materials
- Advanced materials processing for semiconductor, aerospace, and biomedical applications
- Digital manufacturing, process monitoring, and data-driven manufacturing systems
- Biomaterials design, **cytotoxicity**, **biocompatibility**, and toxicity assessment for medical devices and implantable materials

My work bridges **materials science, manufacturing engineering, AI, and biomedical technologies** to develop scalable solutions that accelerate the transition from fundamental research to real-world applications.
